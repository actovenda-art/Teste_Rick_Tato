Pricing questions

Before you choose

Are hosting and domain costs included?

Project pricing covers the design and build. Domain, hosting, premium plugins, booking platforms, or payment-provider charges are confirmed separately because they depend on your chosen tools.

Can we start with one package and add features?

Yes. Packages are starting points. Additional pages, copywriting, photography, video, e-commerce, or integrations can be added to a tailored scope.

How are payments structured?

The payment schedule is agreed before the project starts and documented with the final deliverables, timeline, and revision allowance.

What do revision rounds cover?

Revision rounds cover grouped feedback on the agreed design and content. New functionality or major changes in direction are estimated separately.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Ongoing updates, monitoring, content changes, and growth support can be arranged after the included launch-support period.
