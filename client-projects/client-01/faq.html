Treatment guide

Before you book

Every head of hair is different. These answers are a useful starting point; your consultation provides advice tailored specifically to you.

What is a Brazilian Keratin Treatment?

It is a professional smoothing service that helps control frizz, improve shine and make hair easier to manage. The technique and processing time are selected for your hair type, condition and desired result.

How long do the results last?

Longevity varies with hair type, washing frequency, products, lifestyle and aftercare. The 12 Weeks Blow Dry is designed for results lasting approximately twelve weeks, while your specialist can explain what is realistic for other services.

What is the difference between Keratin, Hair Botox and Nanoplasty?

Keratin focuses on smoothing and frizz control. Hair Botox is primarily a deep reconstruction and nourishment service. Nanoplasty combines smoothing with a reconstruction-focused approach. A consultation is the best way to choose.

Is Brazilian Keratin suitable for every hair type?

It can be adapted for many textures, but suitability depends on hair condition and chemical history. Ane assesses your hair before recommending a service and always places hair integrity first.

Why are some prices listed as “from”?

Product quantity, appointment length and technique depend on hair length, density and condition. Your final service and price are confirmed after assessment.

When can I wash my hair after treatment?

Aftercare differs by treatment and professional product. Follow the instructions given at your appointment, including when to wash and which home-care products to use.

Can I colour my hair before or after a smoothing service?

Timing matters and depends on the colour service, smoothing system and health of your hair. Mention all previous colour and chemical work when booking so the team can recommend a safe sequence.

Can I treat only my fringe?

Yes. The Fringe Treatment is a focused smoothing option priced at €70 and is useful when the front section needs more control than the rest of your hair.

How do I book or change an appointment?

Use the Booksy profile to see live availability and book online. Changes and cancellations should follow the current policy shown during the Booksy booking process.
