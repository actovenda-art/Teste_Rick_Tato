Our story

Care built on experience.

A Brazilian approach to healthy, beautiful hair — refined through more than two decades behind the chair and shared with clients in the heart of Cork.

20+years of expertise
Ane Gonçalves

From Brazilian craft to a welcoming Cork salon.

Ane has spent more than 20 years helping women understand, care for and enjoy their hair. Her specialist knowledge of Brazilian Keratin, reconstruction and smoothing techniques sits at the centre of the salon, alongside colour, cutting, hydration and styling.

At Ane Hair, every service begins with listening. Hair texture, previous chemical services, condition, lifestyle and personal goals all matter. That conversation helps Ane choose the right professional products and technique — and helps clients know exactly what to expect.
